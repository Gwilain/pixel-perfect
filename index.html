About Pixel Perfect

Pixel Perfect is a local-first measurement tool created by Ghislain Gillet.

Your images, screenshots, measurements, swatches, preferences and recent projects stay in your browser storage. The app does not upload your work to a server.

The browser may still load the app files from the site that hosts it, and screen capture uses the browser's native permission prompt.

Capture a tab

The browser will ask you to choose a tab, window, or screen to capture.

Open the target tab first, put it in the state you want, then come back here and choose it from the browser picker.

For more precise full-page captures, use a browser extension such as FireShot, export a PNG, then open or paste it here.
